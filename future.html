THE BETTER DAYS WE HOPE FOR

Despite everything we’ve been through — the highs, the lows, the moments of doubt — I still believe in us. I believe in the love that brought us together, and the possibility of something even stronger ahead. In the future, I hope for more understanding between us — the kind that comes not just from knowing each other’s stories, but from truly listening, without judgment. I hope for more laughter, for days where we don’t take life too seriously and we just enjoy each other, fully and freely. I hope we keep growing — individually and as a couple. That we continue learning how to communicate better, how to support each other when life gets heavy, and how to create space for love even on the hardest days. I dream of a future where we wake up every morning grateful to have each other, where we choose one another over and over, even when it’s not easy. I hope for quiet nights and deep conversations, spontaneous adventures and shared dreams. I hope we build a life that reflects the depth of our connection — full of trust, honesty, and patience. And most of all, I hope that years from now, we can look back at everything we overcame and smile — knowing that every hard moment was worth it because it led us here. No matter what the road looks like ahead, I want it to be with you. That’s what I hope for most.
