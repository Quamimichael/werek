PRESENT TIMES

Right now, our relationship feels like a constant balancing act — some days are better than others, but many times, it feels like we’re barely holding it together. We’ve grown in ways we never imagined, but with that growth comes new challenges. The things we used to easily overlook now feel like walls between us. We find ourselves getting caught in the same arguments, the same miscommunications, and sometimes, the silence between us feels louder than any words we could say. It’s frustrating, because deep down, we know how much we care for each other — but it feels like the weight of daily life is making it harder to connect. There are moments when I question if we’re drifting further apart, and it scares me. I miss the simplicity of when everything seemed easier, when our love felt like it was enough to overcome any obstacle. Now, I feel like we’re constantly trying to figure out how to keep going, how to find the spark we once had. It’s not that I love you any less — in fact, it’s because I love you so deeply that it’s so painful to see us struggle like this. We’re both carrying so much, emotionally and mentally, and sometimes it feels like we’re too tired to keep trying. But even in these tough times, there’s still something that keeps pulling me back to you. Maybe it’s the shared memories, the good times we’ve had, or just the hope that we can get through this. Right now, we’re not perfect, and I don’t know what the future holds for us, but I know that I want to keep fighting for us, for what we’ve built, and for the love I still believe is strong enough to overcome what we’re going through.
